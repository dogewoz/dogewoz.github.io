Setting up your installation

  • If you haven't yet, please set up your copy of Microsoft Windows first, before proceeding with the installation of DOGVERSE.VHS. The game requires a version of Microsoft Windows 7 or later in order to run. Microsoft Windows XP and earlier will NOT work with DOGVERSE.VHS. Windows 7 or later is required.
  • Insert the DOGVERSE.VHS Installation disk, and extract the "dogverse" folder to a location of your choosing. We recommend either the "Documents" folder, or your desktop.
    • we're kidding. it doesn't exist.
  • Click on the "dogverse" folder, and look for a file named "RPG_RT". This is the executable file. Double click on it and you should see the KADOKAWA splash screen.
  • After the KADOKAWA splash screen, you should finally see the main menu. Select "start" to start a new game.

    • Minimum System Requirements

    For the most optimal experience, you will need

  • An x86-64 operating system (required)
  • At least 512 MB of RAM.
  • Around 400 MB of free disk space.
  • A monitor that's at least 1024x768 in High Color mode.
  • An Intel Pentium 4 processor, clocked at 1.5GHz.
  • A sound card, speakers, or a headset.

    • To play the game, you must have the DOGVERSE.VHS Launch Disk inserted into your CD drive in order to launch the game.

    For the best experience, please familiarize yourself with the controls.

  • Arrow keys or H, J, K, L : Move around
  • Z, Space, or Enter : Confirm
  • X, N, B, or Esc : Cancel/Menu

    • You can use a joypad in place of your keyboard! You may need to bind the joypad buttons first. Refer to your joypad's instruction manual for instructions on how to do so.

    Getting started

    Traverse through Oneshot's world and defeat enemies that stand in your way.

    The regions are... the Barrens, the Glen, a small sewer, that leads to the Hometown, which then leads to a retro computer, which then leads to the Refuge, then the Tower.

    In a battle! Help!

    We got you. Here's what you should know.

    In a battle, you can run, or fight.

  • Bash : Deal physical damage to your enemy.
  • Act (short for action): Perform an action.
  • Magic : Cast a magic spell. (Jerry does not have any magic.)
  • Items : Use an item you have in your inventory.
  • Guard : Go on the defense. You'll take less damage for the duration of the turn.
  • Check : See an enemy's info, such as ATK, DEF, and their description.
  • Flee : Escape from the current battle. This might not always work.
