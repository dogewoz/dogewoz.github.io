Jerry the Dog

Jerry the dog is a dog who first appeared in the gun him down video in the Old Youtube-esque Universe, and the main character in all of the known Dogewoz videos (as of August 2023) and the possible main character(s) in the upcoming Dogewoz Productionz movie.

Origins

In real-life, the concept for Jerry the Dog was for a profile picture for a long abandoned account that Dogewoz (me.) owns. On the profile picture of that account, an early Jerry the Dog can be seen. He appears without any eyeballs, and simply had dots as eyes. He was also not drawn with any attire, and simply was fully white.


Universe-wise, Jerry the Dog's backstory is uncertain. But what is known is he most likely came from Canada, which is evidenced by the fact his gameshow takes place in Toronto, the capital of Ontario, a Canadian province. He is depicted as an odd dog, which led him to shooting one of his clones in the gun him down video. In a comic named, Shut up Michael! he is depicted as one of the characters.The comic is not to be considered a part of the Old Youtube-esque Universe. In the mentioned comic, he self-described himself as a Shiba Inu dog, despite the other character, Michael, saying he looks like a Borzoi dog, due to his long nose.
